Treasure Island

Your galleon has reached open water near a rumoured pirate isle. Pirates buried six chests there, but only three hold real gold.

  • Drag the mouse to look, W A S D to walk (arrow keys also walk while no cargo is selected)
  • Climb the stairs at the stern to the quarterdeck and take the ship's wheel to set sail for the island
  • On deck you can also man a cannon for target practice, and visit the captain's cabin (the door under the quarterdeck) to see your trophy cabinet
  • Before you sail: click a loose crate or barrel to select it, then use arrow keys/Q/E/Z/X/Space (bottom-left panel) to lash it onto its marked spot — secure all 3 for a bonus shovel
  • On the island: read all six message bottles, mark your suspects on the treasure map (T), then dig — stroke by stroke — only where you can prove the gold lies
  • Use the compass button (bottom-right) any time to travel between unlocked locations
